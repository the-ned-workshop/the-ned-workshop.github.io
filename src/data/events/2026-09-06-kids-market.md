The Ned Workshop is teaming up with the [Nederland Farmers (Mini) Market](https://www.nederlandfarmersmarket.org) for a Kids Market on Sunday, September 6 from 10am–12pm!

Kiddos ages 8–14 will get to take charge, sell their own handmade creations, and keep 100% of the money they earn.

Come check out everything our AMAZING Kid Vendors are creating:
• 3D Printed Items
• Handmade Jewelry
• Stop Motion Figurines
• Worry Stones
• Sage Bundles
• Paintings

We’ll also have a KIDS BAND on the main stage!

Please bring cash and mounds of encouragement for our mountain young people. See you at the Market!
