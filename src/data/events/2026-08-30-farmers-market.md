Stop by our Farmers Market booth to learn basic embroidery skills. We’ll have supplies to make custom badges, bookmarks, and tiny stuffies.

The skills you learn in this workshop can be used to customize your wardrobe, add charm to your accessories, or make art for your home. The activity of slow stitching brings relaxation and joy and it’s for all ages!

Making is free, donations always appreciated.
