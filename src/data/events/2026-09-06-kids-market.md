The Ned Workshop is partnering with Nederland Farmers (Mini) Market to host a Kids Mini Market on Sunday, September 6 from 10am-12pm.

Our Kids Market aims to let kiddos take charge for a day and make and sell their own creations. 100% of money earned will go to the kids. We're starting small to make sure we get all the details nailed down and hope to do a larger kids market near the holiday season.

Are you a kid aged 8-15 (or do you have a kid) who is interested in selling your makes and wears at Nederlands Mini Farmers Market? If so, reach out to The Ned Workshop using [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfdSSZNMy9x2JvbJD3NPW5ZdqyBi6StS6tjAOlsDsl2OF1jCA/viewform)!

We only have space for 5 vendors this time and are hoping to have a mix of artists, creators, and sellers. With only 3 weeks to go, we want to hear from you ASAP! Please apply by Wednesday, August 19th. Everyone will receive a response by Saturday, August 22.

Don't let the small size of this market keep you from applying - this will give us a good picture of interest for future markets! Read the [Kids Market FAQs](https://nedworkshop.org/kids-market-faqs.pdf) for all the details.

For all you non kiddos - get your cash ready to support the awesome, wild mountain kiddos of Ned!
